Our Academy Story

From one lane to a full aquatic centre.

AQUAFLOW Swimming Academy was founded in 2014 by a small team of national-level swimmers who wanted every child and adult in the city to experience the confidence that comes with swimming well.

What began as two lanes and twelve students has grown into a complete aquatic centre with indoor and outdoor pools, a dedicated kids’ area and more than twenty-five certified coaches.

Our philosophy has never changed: patient coaching, honest progress and a safe, joyful pool for everyone.

  • Founded by national-level swimmers
  • Programs for ages 4 to 70+
  • Community swim days and safety workshops

Safety & Training Standards

Strict standards, every single session.

  • Lifeguards on duty at all times
  • Water quality tested and logged three times daily
  • Coaches hold CPR and first-aid certification
  • Maximum of 8 swimmers per coach
  • Level-based progression with written assessments
  • Parent viewing gallery and regular progress updates
